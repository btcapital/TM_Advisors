Fixed this round

  • --elev-1 on the tint band was rgba(22, 47, 30, …), a digit transposition of the navy ink that produced a green shadow.
  • --fg-4 measured 3.4 to 3.7:1 on light bands, failing AA for six components built on it. Now 0.66 alpha: 4.97:1 on bone, 4.63:1 on the tint.
  • The paper band was missing from eight gold overrides, so full-strength gold rendered at about 2.3:1 on it.
  • The header scrim was pinned to bone, painting a warm ground over the tint band. It now reads the band behind it.
  • Focus rings on the track buttons were clipped by the scroller's overflow-x.
  • The track claimed role="tablist" while showing all six panels at once. Now a labelled scroll region plus a button group.
  • Arrow buttons used disabled, which dropped the focused element out of the tab order mid-interaction. Now aria-disabled.
  • Card fills matched the band below them, draining the separation out of both.
  • Hard-coded hexes replaced with the ink, line, padding and motion tokens they were duplicating.

Open decisions for Bryan

  • The tint band. Champagne (in the gold family) or sage (a stronger third note, but a second hue). One-line swap in theme-bands.css.
  • Card radius. The reference boxes are soft; Meridian's cards are 4 to 6px. Currently 14px as a compromise. If approved, main.css's radii should move with it rather than the site carrying two card languages.
  • The matrix content. The five situations and the discipline mapping are drafted from existing service and bio copy. They are a claim about what the firm does and need sign-off plus a compliance read.
  • The service card footnotes. Drafted from the team bios. Verify each one is accurate.
  • The card right-hand column. Currently an abstract mark. The stronger option is the people who own each service, which needs an owner named for NIL and Family Office.
