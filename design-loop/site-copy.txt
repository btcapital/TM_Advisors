INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY
Portfolio
Architecture

Every portfolio begins with a plan: your objectives, your time horizon, your tax position. From there we build in three layers, each with a distinct job to do. Together they form a single architecture designed to navigate volatility and pursue long-term growth.

Attribute
	Core Diversified Layer 01 · The Foundation	Tactical Overlay Layer 02 · The Complement	Alternative Investments Layer 03 · Qualified Clients
Job to do	Anchor the portfolio	Respond as conditions shift	Diversify beyond public markets
How it is built	Low-cost index and factor-based, globally diversified	Momentum and trend-responsive, with sector rotation	Private credit, real estate, private equity and infrastructure
When it changes	Systematically, on a rebalancing protocol	Dynamically, as relative strength shifts	On the underlying sponsor's timeline
Who it is for	Every client	Every client	Qualified investors only
Liquidity	Daily	Daily	Often limited
Layer 01 of 03
Core
Diversified
THE FOUNDATION
Low-cost index & factor-based implementation
Domestic, international & emerging market exposure
Systematic rebalancing protocols
Ongoing cost and tax-efficiency review

The foundation of every portfolio we build. We allocate by discipline rather than by forecast, with this layer designed to capture broad market returns while holding down cost and uncompensated risk through global diversification.

DESIGNED TO

Serve as the reliable core of the portfolio, seeking consistent long-term growth across full market cycles.

Layer 02 of 03
Tactical
Overlay
THE COMPLEMENT
Momentum & trend-responsive strategies
Sector-specific ETF rotation
Dynamic risk positioning framework
Continuous relative-strength monitoring

Market dislocations can create opportunities. This layer is designed to spot shifts in momentum, value and relative strength across sectors and asset classes, and to respond to them while the core portfolio stays put.

DESIGNED TO

Seek improved risk-adjusted returns in favorable markets, and provide a mechanism for defensive positioning when volatility is elevated.

Layer 03 of 03
Alternative
Investments
QUALIFIED CLIENTS
Private credit & direct lending
Real estate syndications
Private equity & infrastructure access
Independent due diligence on every sponsor

Meaningful diversification often extends beyond public markets. Qualified clients get access to private investments that have historically shown lower correlation to public equities, which is where the diversification benefit is meant to come from.

DESIGNED TO

Pursue income and capital appreciation through channels that have historically shown lower sensitivity to daily public market movements.

THE PROCESS
How a portfolio
gets built.
01
Discovery

We start with your balance sheet, your tax position and what you want the money to do. Recommendations come after we understand all three.

02
Design

We model an allocation across the three layers, sized to your risk tolerance, liquidity needs and time horizon.

03
Implementation

We establish positions with an eye on trading cost, tax lots, and the timing of any concentrated-position unwind.

04
Ongoing Review

We monitor portfolios continuously and rebalance on protocol. We meet as often as you like, and always when something material changes.

Diversification and asset allocation do not ensure a profit or protect against loss in a declining market. Alternative investments are available only to qualified investors, may involve limited liquidity and heightened risk, and are not suitable for every client. All investing involves risk, including the possible loss of principal. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
